GST compliance for retailers: build in-house or buy

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: first draft from desk research, before the team's own shortlist is added
Scope: Trendzo as a software platform that connects retailers and shoppers (retailers are the suppliers; Trendzo is the mediator).
Not tax advice. Legal points come from CBIC-linked and secondary sources (cbic-gst.gov.in and incometaxindia.gov.in could not be read directly). A chartered accountant (CA) should confirm everything in section 8 before decisions are made.

1. Summary and recommendation

  • Trendzo has obligations of its own, not just the retailers'. Shoppers pay through Trendzo's checkout (Razorpay), so Trendzo is an e-commerce operator (ECO) that collects the money. That makes it responsible for TCS at 0.5% per retailer, a monthly GSTR-8 return, TDS at 0.1% on retailer payouts and its own commission invoices. The retailer remains the supplier and owes the tax invoice to the shopper.
  • Most of the building blocks already exist in the code: invoices, gap-free numbering, credit notes, payout maths, month close, a GSTR-1 B2C CSV and a TCS CSV. But several are wrong or unfinished (placeholder issuer GSTIN on commission invoices, GSTR-3B returns an error, no GSTR-8, no TDS data, month close fails from the screen).
  • Do not buy a full GST platform yet. Public pricing for the big suites starts at roughly ₹90,000 a year and runs to ₹3.6-6 lakh a year, and they mainly solve a problem Trendzo does not have yet: retailers above ₹5 crore turnover needing e-invoice and e-way bill for B2B sales.

  • Recommended path (hybrid):

    1. Fix and finish the in-house ledger and documents first. It is the data every filing depends on.
    2. File GSTR-8 with the free government offline utility, or a CA (about ₹2,000-4,000 a month).
    3. Verify retailer GSTINs with a cheap API (about ₹0.40-0.72 a check).
    4. Use a filing service or desktop tool for the TDS return (about ₹2,250 a quarter managed).
    5. Revisit a GST API provider once retailers need e-invoice or e-way bill, or when manual filing becomes the bottleneck. - What changes the answer: whether retailers or Trendzo collect the shopper's money, how many retailers are above ₹5 crore, and how fast the legal rules keep changing (five changes in about two years, section 4).

2. Where Trendzo sits as a mediator

Trendzo supplies software and takes a commission. The goods are sold by the retailer. The table shows who owes what, assuming Trendzo's checkout collects the shopper's payment.

Obligation Owed by Frequency Trendzo's role
Tax invoice to the shopper (goods) Retailer Per order May generate it on the retailer's behalf in the retailer's name and GSTIN, with a written authorisation and a separate numbering series per GSTIN. The retailer stays liable.
Bill of supply (composition dealers, exempt goods) Retailer Per order Same: generate on behalf, using the right document type per retailer.
Credit note on a return Retailer Per return Generate on behalf; also net returns in GSTR-8.
Commission, platform fee, delivery fee invoice Trendzo Per order or per month (within 30 days) Issue under Trendzo's own GSTIN, 18% GST. The place of supply for a registered retailer is the retailer's location.
TCS under GST s.52 at 0.5% of each retailer's net taxable supplies (returns netted) Trendzo Monthly Collect from the retailer payout; track per retailer GSTIN.
GSTR-8 return Trendzo By the 10th of the next month Prepare and file; also the annual statement (GSTR-9B). Retailers then accept the TCS credit.
TDS under income tax, old s.194-O (now s.393(1) of the Income-tax Act 2025) at 0.1% Trendzo Deduct at credit or payment; quarterly return Deduct from payouts; file Form 140 (earlier 26Q); issue Form 131 (earlier 16A). Resident individuals or HUFs up to ₹5 lakh a year who give PAN or Aadhaar are exempt.
E-invoice (IRN) Retailer, only if its annual turnover is above ₹5 crore Per B2B invoice May generate on the retailer's behalf. B2C invoices are out of scope, so most retailers need none.
E-way bill Consignor (retailer) Per consignment above ₹50,000 May fill Part A with authorisation.
Registration of sellers Retailer Once Enforce: compulsory when the ECO collects TCS, except small intra-state sellers with an enrolment ID.
Records for 72 months, edit log All parties Continuous Keep records; answer officer requests within 15 working days.

Points that matter for design:

  • Section 9(5), where the platform pays the GST itself, does not cover goods or fashion. It applies to services such as restaurants and, since 22 Sep 2025, local delivery by unregistered riders at 18%. If Trendzo's riders are unregistered, the delivery fee needs a CA's view.
  • Composition dealers may sell goods through an ECO (since 1 Oct 2023), but Trendzo must block their inter-state orders, collect TCS and report them in GSTR-8.
  • Small unregistered sellers can sell only inside their own state, using an enrolment ID. No TCS on them, but they appear in GSTR-8 Table 3.1. They cannot charge GST. There is no exemption for small inter-state sellers of goods. A single-state registration scheme is on the GST Council agenda for 7 Oct 2026.
  • If retailers collected the shoppers' money directly, there would be no TCS and no GSTR-8, but TDS would still apply. That is a design choice with large consequences, so it needs a CA's confirmation.

3. What Trendzo already builds in-house

From a read-only review of the backend and web portal:

Exists and works

  • Tax invoices to shoppers on delivery, supplementary invoices when a held item is kept, credit notes on successful refunds, and counter-sale invoices or bills of supply in POS.
  • Per-order commission invoices (SAC 9985 at 18%) issued on delivery.
  • Gap-free numbering per legal entity, financial year and series, with PDFs stored.
  • Payout maths: commission, 18% GST on commission, TCS, refunds, holds and adjustments.
  • Month close that writes statements, a GSTR-1 B2C CSV and a TCS CSV; a retailer GST summary and HSN summary with CSV export.

Known defects and gaps

  • GSTR-3B is not built (the endpoint returns an error); there is no GSTR-8; there is no TDS ledger and no Form 140 or 131 data.
  • The commission invoice issuer is the placeholder PLATFORM-GSTIN; it uses the retailer's numbering series; the CGST/SGST versus IGST choice uses the shopper's state; commission is not reversed on returns.
  • The month-close button sends a period format the API rejects, so it fails from the screen; closing does not lock the period.
  • TCS defaults to 1% where the law says 0.5%, is added to the shopper's invoice total although checkout does not charge it, and is calculated on different bases in the invoice and the payout.
  • Composition dealers get a tax invoice online; buyer GSTIN is never captured, so no B2B; shoppers cannot fetch their invoices.
  • GSTIN is checked for length only. No e-invoice and no e-way bill.
  • A failed invoice is only logged; nothing retries. The retailer statement PDF button is disabled and its holds and TCS figures show zero.

Sizes are relative effort (S under a week, M 1-3 weeks, L over 3 weeks, for one developer who knows the code), not estimates.

Rules keep changing, and each change is engineering and CA time:

  • TCS rate cut from 1% to 0.5% (10 Jul 2024).
  • TDS rate cut from 1% to 0.1% (1 Oct 2024).
  • Local delivery by unregistered riders brought under s.9(5) at 18% (22 Sep 2025).
  • Credit notes tied to the buyer's input credit reversal through the Invoice Management System (1 Oct 2025).
  • Income-tax Act 2025 (1 Apr 2026): old section 194-O became s.393(1); Form 26Q became Form 140 and Form 16A became Form 131.
  • Pending: a single-state registration scheme for small ECO sellers (Council meeting 7 Oct 2026).

5. Third-party options by layer

All prices were read on public pages on 2026-10-03 and are in rupees. "Quote on request" means no public price. Whether prices include GST was often not stated.

5.1 GSTIN, PAN and bank verification

  • Appyflow: GSTIN lookup, ₹0.50 a request (₹0.40 if a recharge is above ₹1,000), credits never expire, 50 free requests. https://appyflow.in/verify-gst/
  • gstinapi.com / gstinapi.in: ₹0.50 a check, ₹0.40 at 2,500+ credits; the second site quotes ₹0.40-0.80 and 100 free lookups (its comparison page is self-promotional). https://gstinapi.com/pricing
  • GSTZen GST Validator: ₹3,500 a year for 5,000 calls, ₹5,000 for 10,000, ₹8,250 for 25,000. https://gstzen.in/gst-validator-api-pricing-details
  • Eko EPS: GSTIN from ₹0.72 a verification; PAN and bank too. https://eps.eko.in/products/gst-verification-api
  • Quote on request: Cashfree Secure ID (GSTIN, PAN, bank penny-drop; ₹100 trial credits), Signzy (₹5,000 sandbox credits), Surepass, Karza (Perfios), IDfy, Attestr. Setu offers bank verification; a "₹3 per verification" figure appeared only in search snippets, so it is not verified.
  • GSTN enrolment-ID checks for unregistered sellers are available only through a GSP.
  • Verdict: buy. At a few hundred retailers the cost is under ₹1,000 a year.

5.2 E-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill APIs

  • NIC portals: free, but a direct API is for very large taxpayers only (the threshold is stated as ₹100 crore on one NIC page and ₹500 crore elsewhere). Everyone else goes through a GSP. https://einv-apisandbox.nic.in/onboarding.html
  • IRIS IRP-6 and Cygnet IRP-3: government-authorised e-invoice portals; basic generation free (IRIS premium services "to be announced"). https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/pricing-plan/
  • GSTZen: e-invoice 18 paise an invoice with a ₹50,000 a year minimum (invoices kept 10 days), or 50 paise with a ₹25,000 minimum (kept 7 years); signing certificate ₹15,000 a year. E-way bill 18 paise with a ₹50,000 minimum per GSTIN, or 35 paise with ₹25,000 per GSTIN. https://gstzen.in/e-invoicing-api-integration-pricing
  • Adaequare: IRN 0.25 a transaction, IRN with e-way bill 0.40, custom payloads 0.80, plus GSTR-1 transfer 1.50. Its page had an expired certificate when read, and the currency symbol did not display, so INR is assumed. https://ugsp.adaequare.com/e-invoice-api
  • Verdict: buy, only when retailers above ₹5 crore sell to businesses or ship large consignments. Prefer per-transaction pricing; avoid per-GSTIN minimums with many small retailers.

5.3 GST suites and API providers (GSPs)

  • ClearTax (Clear): suite "starting at ₹7,777 a month"; API per-call price on request. Says it is a GSP/ASP. Each GSTIN needs its own NIC API credentials. https://cleartax.in/s/e-invoicing-gst-compliance-solution/
  • Adaequare: GST API plans ₹50,000 a month including 2,00,000 calls, or ₹30,000 a month including 1,00,000 calls; "Enriched" plan ₹50,000 a month including 500 GSTINs. https://ugsp.adaequare.com/gst-apis
  • WhiteBooks: two pages conflict: "₹5,999 to ₹24,999" and "startup tier from ₹14,999 a month". Explicit multi-GSTIN support with delegated consent. https://whitebooks.in/about/pricing/
  • Quote on request: Masters India (also a GSP), MasterGST (Tera Software), IRIS GSP, WeP, TaxPro GSP, Cygnet GSP.
  • Vayana: not verified; a reseller lists ₹40,000-75,000 a year plus integration fees.
  • Sandbox.co.in (Quicko): 200+ APIs including GSTIN, returns, e-invoice, PAN and bank; a technology provider, not a GSP; 14-day trial; plan prices are a dynamic calculator and were not readable. https://sandbox.co.in/pricing
  • India Compliance (ERPNext): open-source GST app, free on Frappe Cloud; credit price not verified. https://indiacompliance.app/
  • Verdict: defer. These matter when many retailers need returns, e-invoice and e-way bill through your platform. Ask any shortlisted provider whether it stores each retailer's credentials with consent and whether pricing is per call or per GSTIN.

5.4 GSTR-8 and TCS tools

  • GSTN GSTR-8 Offline Utility: free government download; you fill a CSV or Excel and upload it. Good if Trendzo exports a retailer-wise CSV each month.
  • Clear GST (ClearTax): GSTR-8 preparation, many GSTINs, template import, TCS challans; quote on request.
  • Taxilla: matches TCS to order and settlement data, seller GSTIN checks, approvals, audit logs; enterprise, quote on request. https://www.taxilla.com/blog/gstr-8-filing
  • IRIS Sapphire, Cygnet Tax: report GSTR-8; quote on request (Cygnet not verified, its site did not load).
  • Gen GST (SAG Infotech): desktop ₹5,000 install and ₹2,000 a year; online e-filing ₹25,000 and ₹10,000; all-in-one online ₹10,000 and ₹7,000, plus 18% GST. GSTR-8 appears only in a third-party listing. https://saginfotech.com/PriceCalculator.aspx
  • Not confirmed to support GSTR-8 for operators: Zoho Books, TallyPrime, Avalara India, Masters India (no product page), Vayana (no public endpoint).
  • Verdict: use the free utility or a CA now; look at Clear or Taxilla only at thousands of sellers or for API filing.

5.5 TDS (194-O) returns and certificates

  • Sandbox (Quicko): TDS calculation, PAN and TAN checks, Form 16A and 26Q APIs; plan price not readable. https://sandbox.co.in/tds
  • Gen e-TDS: ₹3,500 install and ₹1,500 a year, plus GST. https://saginfotech.com/PriceCalculator.aspx
  • Winman TDS: list ₹13,300; ₹7,490 only for existing Winman licence holders; the price list is undated. https://www.winmansoftware.com/files/Software_pricelist_winman.pdf
  • KDK Spectrum Cloud TDS: bulk Form 16/16A, TRACES fetch, Form 26Q/140; price not on the vendor page (a third party says ₹6,300 a year, not verified). https://www.kdksoftware.com/spectrum-cloud/tds/
  • TDSMAN: desktop and cloud editions; rupee amounts not readable. https://www.tdsman.com/pricing.asp
  • Managed filing: EZTax Form 140 / 26Q ₹2,249 plus GST a quarter, including Form 131 certificates (https://eztax.in/form26q-tds-return); ClearTax expert TDS from ₹2,500 for 24Q and 26Q (https://cleartax.in/tds).
  • Check before buying: only KDK and EZTax mention the renamed Forms 140 and 131.

5.6 Managed CA and compliance services

  • Patron Accounting (indicative, per its page): GSTR-8 only ₹1,999 a month per GSTIN; GSTR-8 + GSTR-1 + GSTR-3B ₹3,999 a month; reconciliation ₹2,999 a month; section 9(5) add-on ₹1,999 a month; annual return ₹4,999; annual package ₹39,999; 20% off for 5+ GSTINs. TDS 194-O is not mentioned. https://www.patronaccounting.com/gst-returns-for-e-commerce-operator
  • IndiaFilings, TaxBuddy: no prices shown.
  • Note: GSTR-8 is filed under Trendzo's own GSTIN, so "per GSTIN" here means one or two GSTINs, not one per retailer.

5.7 Payout splitting and retailer-side accounting

  • Cashfree Easy Split and Razorpay Route: split payments and pay vendors; reconciliation reports. Neither page says it computes TCS or TDS natively. Prices not checked. https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/split/billing/configuration, https://razorpay.com/route/
  • Zoho Books (per organisation a month, billed annually): Free; Standard ₹749; Professional ₹1,499; Premium ₹2,999; Elite ₹4,999; Ultimate ₹7,999. E-invoice and e-way bill from Standard up. API 1,000-10,000 calls a day by plan. https://www.zoho.com/in/books/pricing/
  • TallyPrime: Silver ₹22,500 lifetime or ₹750 a month; Gold ₹67,500 or ₹2,250 a month. Integration is XML against a running Tally install, not cloud REST (third-party source). https://tallysolutions.com/buy-tally/
  • Cygnet Invoicing Tool: free plan; Standard ₹9,999 a year (3,000 invoices, up to 10 GSTINs); Premium ₹14,999 (10,000 invoices, up to 20 GSTINs). https://invoicingtool.cygnettaxtech.com/pricing/
  • Swipe: list prices not verified. Razorpay Invoices: the API can create only non-GST invoices, so it cannot make GST invoices.
  • Verdict: these are for retailers to run their own books. Paying for them per retailer does not scale (see the scenario table).

What the research did not find: a public vendor case study of a marketplace; how Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho or Myntra run TCS and TDS internally (only Amazon's blog says Amazon files GSTR-8).

8. Risks and open questions for a CA

  1. Does Trendzo collect the shopper's money? The code says yes (Razorpay checkout). If yes, TCS, GSTR-8 and compulsory seller registration follow. If retailers collected directly, GST TCS would fall away but TDS would not.
  2. SAC code for the commission. No official mapping exists; 998599 / 9985 is common practice, 9962 (retail on a fee basis) is arguable, software subscriptions may fall under 9973 or 9983.
  3. Income-tax Act 2025 details. Payment code (secondary sources disagree between 1035 and 1037) and whether circulars issued under the 1961 Act carry over.
  4. GST Council meeting on 7 Oct 2026. A single-state registration scheme for small sellers may change onboarding rules.
  5. Unregistered enrolled sellers. What document they should issue, since they cannot charge GST.
  6. Delivery fee and unregistered riders. Since 22 Sep 2025, local delivery by unregistered riders through an ECO is taxed under s.9(5) at 18%; check whether this affects Trendzo's riders.
  7. Data protection. The DPDP Rules apply mostly from 13 May 2027 (a proposal to shorten the timeline is unconfirmed). Sole-proprietor GSTINs embed PAN and invoices carry shoppers' names, so they are personal data; GST and income-tax retention duties override erasure. Whether Trendzo is a data fiduciary or processor for retailers' invoice data is unclear.
  8. Penalties. Failing to collect or deposit TCS, or letting an ineligible seller sell, can cost ₹10,000 or the tax involved, whichever is higher.
  9. Prices not verified: Vayana, Swipe, Setu, Sandbox plans, India Compliance credits, TDSMAN, KDK, IndiaFilings, TaxBuddy, Razorpay Route, Cashfree Easy Split; the conflict between two WhiteBooks pages; Adaequare's currency symbol; GSTZen's GSP status; whether each GSTR-8 and TDS product has been updated for the new Forms 140 and 131.

9. Recommended path

Phase 0: fix what exists (in-house)

  • Real platform GSTIN, series and place of supply on commission invoices; reverse commission on returns.
  • One TCS rate (0.5%) and one base, charged to retailers' payouts, not to shoppers.
  • Fix the month-close period format, lock closed periods, add retries for failed invoices.
  • Bill of supply for composition dealers online; capture buyer GSTIN for B2B.

Phase 1: close the platform's own filings

  • Seller-wise TCS and TDS ledgers; export a GSTR-8 CSV; file with the free offline utility or a CA.
  • TDS: produce Form 140 / 131 data; file with a managed service or a desktop tool.
  • Buy GSTIN verification (cheap API) at onboarding and on change requests.

Phase 2: help retailers file

  • Retailer GST pack (invoices, GSTR-1 and HSN data, TCS and TDS certificates) and a read-only CA role.

Phase 3: buy where it hurts

  • Only if retailers above ₹5 crore or large consignments appear: an e-invoice and e-way bill API on per-transaction pricing.
  • Only if filing on behalf of retailers becomes a product: a GSP with delegated credentials.

Decision gates: CA confirmation of section 8 items; number of retailers above ₹5 crore turnover; whether manual GSTR-8 and TDS filing takes more staff time than a managed service costs.
